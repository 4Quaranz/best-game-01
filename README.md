MOTHLIGHT — you are a moth in a night garden. Steer with the mouse or WASD, collect glowing pollen to feed your lantern light, dodge the bats that chase you, and do not linger in spider webs. Your glow is your light meter and your score multiplier: bright light earns up to x3, and every 15 pollen the night gets harder. Three hits and the light goes out.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. To hand the game in:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
